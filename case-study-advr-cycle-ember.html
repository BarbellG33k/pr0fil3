AI Adoption / Cultural Transformation

A-D-V-R Cycle

Architect -> Direct -> Validate -> Repeat

Executive Summary

A high-speed workflow created for the organization's first AI Hackathon, positioning the developer as architect and AI as apprentice so teams could experience safe, structured acceleration rather than uncontrolled code generation.

Operating Pattern

Architect: define the blueprint and constraints before execution.

Direct: give the AI a precise, bounded task from the blueprint.

Validate: review output before allowing the next cycle.

Repeat: compound progress through small approved iterations.

Origin & Leadership Mechanism

Created to move teams from AI skepticism to embedded practice through a practical shared workflow.
