Process Innovation

Definition Pipeline

Idea -> Refinement -> PRD -> TRD -> Plan -> Tasks: the intake half of the AI-augmented SDLC, BMAD-influenced and homegrown.

Pattern

AI made execution cheap, but intake-from idea to actionable work-stayed heavy, slow, and committee-shaped. Definition became the bottleneck the tools could not touch.

Mechanism

A staged definition chain an individual engineer can drive with an AI workspace: develop the idea, refine it, produce a PRD, then static technical artifacts (TRDs), then a plan, then tasks.

Outcome

Intake compresses from committee cycle to single-engineer-plus-agents flow, and every artifact doubles as context for downstream agents-execution starts faster and starts smarter.

Executive Summary

When AI collapsed the cost of execution, the constraint moved upstream: the slowest part of delivery was no longer building the thing but deciding what to build, precisely enough to build it. Traditional intake-ideation through committees, heavy product definition cycles-was shaped for a world where building was expensive and thinking was cheap. AI inverts that.

Started with BMAD as an early reference-an agentic lifecycle with specialized agents per phase-and adapted rather than adopted it wholesale. With the chief architect and staff engineers, the pipeline evolved into the organization's own definition chain: an idea is developed, refined, turned into a PRD, then into deliberately static technical artifacts (TRDs), then a plan, then tasks. Each stage is shaped so one engineer with an AI workspace can produce what once took a committee, and the artifacts are engineered twice over-they brief humans and they feed agents downstream. The pipeline has gone through continuous iterations of refinement, each informed by team feedback and knowledge-transfer clinics.

Problem Recognized

  • Intake and definition were the heavy, slow half of the delivery cycle-the part AI had not yet touched.
  • Ideas lost fidelity on the way from product to engineering, and the loss was expensive once build became cheap.
  • Definition artifacts were written for humans only-agents received scraps of context.
  • Off-the-shelf agentic frameworks needed adaptation to the organization's products, constraints, and compliance context.

Strategic Position

  • When execution is cheap, definition quality is the throughput limit-invest there.
  • Adopt frameworks as references; build the pipeline your organization will actually run.
  • Artifacts are context: PRDs and TRDs brief both people and agents.
  • One empowered engineer with an AI workspace can replace a committee's intake cycle.

Leadership Mechanisms

BMAD Lineage

BMAD evaluated early as a reference model for agentic lifecycles-adapted deliberately rather than adopted wholesale.

Stage-Gate Artifact Chain

Idea -> refinement -> PRD -> TRD -> plan -> tasks: each stage produces an artifact that gates the next.

Artifact-as-Context Design

PRDs and TRDs written to brief humans and feed agents-technical artifacts kept deliberately static and precise.

Intake Compression

Definition cycles compressed from committee cadence to a single engineer driving the chain with an AI workspace.

IC Empowerment

Individual contributors given the method and tooling to own definition end to end-judgment scaled, not just typing.

Iterative Refinement

The pipeline evolves through chief architect and staff/principal engineer partnership, with team feedback folded into every iteration.

Transferable Operating Principles

  • The bottleneck moves-lead it, don't chase it.
  • Borrow frameworks; own your pipeline.
  • Write artifacts for two readers: the human and the agent.
  • Static technical artifacts are a feature, not a flaw-agents need precision.
  • Intake speed is a competitive advantage once build is cheap.
  • Process refinements compound when feedback loops are designed in.
