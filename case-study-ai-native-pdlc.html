Product / Engineering Transformation

AI-Native Product Development Lifecycle

Signal -> Context -> Decision -> Execution -> Memory

Executive Summary

A 5-stage PDLC that treats AI agents as first-class participants and addresses the core shift created by AI: implementation becomes cheaper, so product definition quality becomes the strategic constraint.

Operating Pattern

Signal: capture the business/product trigger with enough fidelity to reason about value.

Context: curate source material for humans and AI agents.

Decision: validate direction and tradeoffs before build.

Execution: feed structured context into engineering and AI workflows.

Memory: preserve decisions, artifacts, and learnings for future cycles.

Origin & Leadership Mechanism

Created and piloted through a structured proving session using Cold-Read Validation to test artifact quality before build.
