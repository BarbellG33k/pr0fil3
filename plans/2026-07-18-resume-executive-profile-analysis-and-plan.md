# V. Claim-confidence and attribution policy

## Confidence levels

Every future claim should be assigned one level:

1. **Verified:** Supported by an authoritative document, dashboard, public source, or formal record.
2. **Directional:** Based on credible internal trend data or estimates but not audited.
3. **Recollection:** Based on memory and awaiting corroboration.
4. **Unresolved:** Conflicting evidence exists; not eligible for publication.

## Confirmed directional metrics

The following are all **directionally accurate estimates**, not audited measurements:

- Approximately 25% reduction in lead time for changes
- Approximately 40% improvement in mean time to recovery
- Approximately 30% reduction in defect rework
- Approximately 3x increase in meaningful code output
- Approximately 35% broader team-efficiency improvement

### Publication rule

- Use “approximately,” “internal trend data indicated,” or “directionally.”
- Record the period, population, definition, source, and confounding changes.
- Do not call these “documented” or independently verified.
- Do not present all five in one dense bullet.
- Prefer lead time, defect rework, MTTR, product adoption, and customer outcomes over lines of code.
- If code output is retained, label it a throughput indicator rather than productivity.

## Attribution verbs

Use the narrowest accurate verb:

- **Built / implemented:** Guillermo personally created the work.
- **Architected:** Guillermo directly designed the system or model.
- **Led / directed:** Guillermo held accountability for delivery through others.
- **Sponsored:** Guillermo provided executive authority, resources, and protection.
- **Co-created:** Material design ownership was shared.
- **Coached / guided:** Leaders or teams owned implementation with Guillermo’s direction.
- **Contributed:** Guillermo materially participated without owning the outcome.
- **Participated:** Guillermo was involved but did not own design or delivery.

The 60/10/30 AI-product role mix should never appear as a public percentage. It should produce product-specific combinations of “led engineering delivery,” “contributed directly to architecture,” and “helped establish governance.”
