# IX. Content and evidence rules

## Attribution verbs

Use the narrowest accurate verb:

- **Built / implemented:** Guillermo personally created it
- **Architected:** Guillermo directly designed it
- **Led / directed:** Guillermo held delivery accountability through others
- **Established:** Guillermo put the mechanism or structure in place
- **Sponsored:** Guillermo supplied authority, resources, or protection
- **Co-created:** material design ownership was shared
- **Coached / guided:** another leader or team owned implementation
- **Contributed:** Guillermo materially participated without owning the result
- **Participated:** involvement without ownership or design authority

Editorial review may vary repeated verbs, but attribution precision always outranks rhetorical variety.

## Outcome confidence

- **Verified:** authoritative record or corroboration
- **Directional:** credible estimate or internal trend, qualified publicly
- **Recollection:** owner-confirmed but awaiting corroboration
- **Unresolved:** conflicting evidence; not publishable
- **Planned:** intended future outcome, never phrased as achieved

Resume bullets should connect action to a supported result, but results do not always require a number. Audit every figure highlighted by external reviews, including `3-4x meaningful code output` and `35%+ efficiency improvement`, for definition, period, attribution, confidence, and public safety. Remove or qualify any figure that fails the evidence-ledger standard.

## AI containment

- Experity-specific AI frameworks remain in Experity.
- Current independent AI product work is separate and requires its own proof.
- Earlier employers demonstrate leadership, architecture, engineering discipline, modernization, and learning, not retroactive AI leadership.

## Identity language

Use identity claims only when the artifact also supplies evidence:

- People-first
- Growth-minded
- Leader of leaders
- Builder
- Innovator
- Engineering practitioner
- Continuous learner
- Force for improvement

Avoid unsupported labels:

- Visionary
- Genius
- World-class
- Transformational leader without scope
- Thought leader without published or externally adopted work
- Entrepreneur without validated ownership and activity

The content should create those impressions through proof rather than state them as praise.
