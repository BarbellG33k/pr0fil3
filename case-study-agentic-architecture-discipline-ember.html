AI Architecture / Technical Strategy

Agentic Architecture Discipline

Definition-First, Then Iteration

Executive Summary

A discipline for evaluating agentic approaches through cost-to-value, definition quality, and architectural fit instead of trend-following. It protects teams from adopting AI patterns before the problem and context are sufficiently defined.

Operating Pattern

Definition precedes iteration: unclear product thinking cannot be solved by faster implementation.

Cost-to-value governs tool choice: agentic work must justify complexity and review overhead.

Context is architecture: AI output quality depends on curated inputs and constraints.

Human accountability remains explicit: AI can execute, but engineers own decisions and outcomes.

Origin & Leadership Mechanism

Developed through practitioner evaluation of AI tooling and applied across product, integration, and platform transformation initiatives. This discipline is described here as an operating principle applied consistently across initiatives rather than tied to a single dated artifact or decision.
